# Architectural invariants

1. A source assertion is a claim with provenance, not truth.
2. Assertions are preserved before entity resolution; unresolved identity is valid state.
3. Entity resolution precedes canonical state; reconciliation chooses governing claims explicitly.
4. Every material derived claim retains a drill-down chain to source structure and artifact.
5. Deterministic arithmetic and state queries do not depend on an LLM.
6. Projections are rebuildable from canonical records and assertion history.
7. False merges are more costly than unresolved identities.
8. Synthetic/public data only; no operational or customer claims without evidence.
