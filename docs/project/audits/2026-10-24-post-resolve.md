## Direct architectural finding and next proposal

The current bom-table package names `procurement.reconcile/explicit-source-precedence@1`.
Its legacy `reconcile_lines` algorithm chooses a source and sums its rows. ADR023 and the ratified
`procurement-governing-claims/v1` policy instead require predicate-specific approval, effective
intervals and explicit supersession. Merely wrapping the legacy algorithm as a seventh executor
would not demonstrate adoption of that ratified governing policy.

The recommended next core proposal is a bounded required-quantity RECONCILE operation consuming
validated retained RESOLVE producers, preserving all sources and abstentions, and using existing
policy v1. This is a proposed direction, not an approved policy change or seven-stage delivery.
The alternative is a separate explicit RESOLVE job-profile adoption; listener/worker infrastructure
and new resolution algorithms remain independent work. The owner has been asked to choose; no
new product implementation is authorized or claimed by this orientation record.
