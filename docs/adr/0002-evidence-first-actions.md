# ADR 0002: Evidence-first, approval-gated actions

## Decision

All recommendations show supporting evidence and uncertainty; external side effects require policy evaluation and human approval unless explicitly allowlisted.

## Context

An incorrect answer is recoverable; an incorrect order change, vendor communication, or schedule update may not be.

## Consequences

Early workflows are slower, but trust, reversibility, and auditability improve. Autonomy can expand only after evaluation supports it.
